Smart Automation

Automation that takes repetitive admin off your team's plate — bookings, invoicing, follow-ups, stock and reporting — built around the tools you already use.

Small Businesses

(e.g. local shops, salons, freelancers, small online stores)

Automation Solutions

  • Client Booking & Follow-up System — automate customer bookings, confirmations and reminders using n8n linked with WhatsApp or Gmail. Solves: missed appointments and manual scheduling.
  • Invoice & Payment Tracking — automatically send invoices and track payments using Airtable, Google Sheets and PayPal/Stripe. Solves: time wasted on billing and financial errors.
  • Social Media Posting Bot (AI-Powered) — AI drafts and schedules social media posts using your business info and current trends. Solves: inconsistent posting and a weak online presence.

AI Marketing

  • AI Email Campaigns — personalised messages written for each customer group.
  • AI Ad Copywriting — ad content drafted and tested faster.

What You Save

  • Less time on admin like bookings, reminders and invoicing.
  • Fewer manual tasks and, in many cases, fewer separate software subscriptions.

Medium Businesses

(e.g. agencies, e-commerce stores, logistics, beauty studios)

Automation Solutions

  • CRM & Lead Automation — automatically capture leads from forms, WhatsApp or email into a CRM (Airtable/HubSpot). Solves: lost leads and slow response times.
  • Inventory & Order Management — use n8n to sync stock between your online store, POS and supplier systems. Solves: out-of-stock issues and double-selling.
  • Employee Workflow Automation — auto-assign tasks and update Airtable/Notion when a new order or client request comes in. Solves: team communication breakdowns.

AI Marketing

  • AI Predictive Marketing — analyse customer data to find your best-selling products and top markets.
  • AI Chatbots — handle FAQs and product recommendations on WhatsApp or your website.

What You Save

  • Repetitive admin handled automatically, so a smaller team can manage a bigger workload.
  • Leads and orders followed up the moment they come in, not when someone gets to them.

Large Business / Enterprise Automation

Ideal for: banks, SaaS companies, corporate retailers, logistics groups and enterprise clients.

Setup (Once-Off)

R35,000 – R80,000+ depending on data complexity. Includes:

  • Full AI automation infrastructure (n8n + API integrations)
  • Custom MCP server — hosts AI agents securely inside the company network
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system — connects your internal documents, policies, FAQs and reports to an AI assistant that answers staff or customer queries from verified company knowledge
  • Automated dashboards for analytics and performance tracking

What the RAG System Does

  • Brings company data (PDFs, Airtable, CRM, Notion, internal docs) into one AI knowledge base.
  • Employees or customers can chat with that data through a chatbot, Slack or a web app.
  • Every answer is sourced from your internal documents, so it stays accurate, private and secure.

Business Benefits

  • Saves time: staff find internal information in seconds instead of digging through folders and inboxes.
  • Cuts support load: common questions are answered instantly, so your team handles the ones that need a person.
  • Improves decision-making: real-time AI dashboards replace slow manual reporting.
  • Boosts efficiency: connects to n8n so answers can trigger workflows — e.g. “Show all unpaid invoices” and the follow-up is automated.
