Project Overview

Max Meal needed a point-of-sale system built for speed on the shop floor across multiple outlets, without staff wrestling with slow logins or disconnected online orders.

Solutions Implemented

  • Outlet and staff-name selection with fast PIN sign-in
  • Touch-friendly checkout built for high-volume service
  • WooCommerce integration to keep online and in-store orders in sync
  • Manager sign-in for oversight across outlets

Results Achieved

Staff can sign in and start serving customers in seconds at any outlet, while online orders placed through WooCommerce flow straight into the same system as in-store sales.
